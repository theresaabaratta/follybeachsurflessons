About This Directory

FollyBeachSurfLessons.com is a small, independent directory of every company and instructor teaching surf lessons on Folly Beach, South Carolina. Each listing links straight to the business so you can compare and book directly.

We don't take bookings, sell lessons, or rank schools. Listings are free and based on publicly available information. Prices, schedules, and meeting spots change by season, so confirm details with the school before you go.

Keeping Listings Accurate

Surf schools open, close, and move. If a listing is out of date or your business is missing, let us know and we'll update it.
